🐍 Python Adventure

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Python Adventure is an educational app designed to teach Python programming to children aged 9–12. We built it with privacy as a priority — we do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

✓ No accounts   ✓ No tracking   ✓ No ads   ✓ No data collection   ✓ COPPA compliant

What We Collect

Nothing. Python Adventure does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Payment information

What Stays on Your Device

The only data Python Adventure saves is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your chosen avatar and display name
  • Lesson progress and completed modules
  • Star count and earned badges
  • Daily goal and streak count
  • Parent Zone PIN (if set)

Internet Use

Python Adventure requires an internet connection on first use to load the Skulpt Python engine (skulpt.org). After the first load, the engine is cached on your device. No personal data is sent during this process.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This app is designed for children. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not require account creation or sign-up
  • We do not show advertisements
  • We do not allow in-app purchases
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

Parent Zone

The optional Parent Zone feature is PIN-protected and stores progress summaries locally on the device only. No information from the Parent Zone is transmitted anywhere.

Data Deletion (COPPA)

Since all data is stored only on your device, parents and guardians can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Inside the app: Settings → Reset Progress
  • Completely: Uninstall the app from your device

There is no account or server-side data — deletion is instant and complete. To request confirmation or ask questions, contact us at Jge.golden@gmail.com

Third Parties

Python Adventure does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The only external resource used is the open-source Skulpt library from skulpt.org to run Python code locally on your device.
